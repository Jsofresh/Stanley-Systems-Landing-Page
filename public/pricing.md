# Stanley Systems Pricing

Canonical HTML page: https://stanley-systems.com/pricing

Stanley Systems pricing starts with the $97 Cash Flow Assessment. The assessment finds where calls, invoices, follow-ups, reviews, referrals, and past customers are leaking before choosing a build path.

Current public pricing includes:

- Cash Flow Assessment: $97 one-time assessment.
- Systems Installation Sprint: starts at $1,500 after the assessment.
- Monthly Control: quoted after the Sprint when ongoing support is needed. Pricing depends on the number of workflows, tools, and support needs.

The strongest path for most buyers is to start with the Cash Flow Assessment, confirm the leak, then install the right system instead of guessing. Monthly Control is optional after launch and is not sold by itself.
