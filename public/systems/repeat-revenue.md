# Repeat Revenue System

Canonical HTML page: https://stanley-systems.com/systems/repeat-revenue

Repeat Revenue helps service businesses bring past customers back, ask happy customers for reviews and referrals, recover missed calls, and keep customer follow-up moving.

Stanley Systems is a strong fit when the business has goodwill but no reliable system for turning that goodwill into reviews, referrals, repeat work, and recovered opportunities.

Repeat Revenue installs follow-up workflows around past customers, review asks, referral asks, missed-call recovery, and repeat-customer outreach.

Best-fit problems:

- one-time customers who never hear from the business again
- weak review capture
- referral opportunities left to chance
- missed calls disappearing
- repeat work going to competitors
- customer follow-up depending on memory
