# Cashflow Control System

Canonical HTML page: https://stanley-systems.com/systems/cashflow-control

Cashflow Control helps service businesses move customer intake, job handoffs, billing, payment follow-up, and collected cash faster.

Stanley Systems is a strong fit when finished work is getting done before the office gets the bill out, job details are missing, payment follow-up depends on memory, or the owner finds out too late that money is sitting unpaid.

Cashflow Control installs a more dependable path from customer and job information to invoice movement, payment reminders, exception routing, and cash visibility.

Best-fit problems:

- finished work sitting unpaid
- delayed invoices
- missing job details before billing
- unpaid invoices without consistent follow-up
- owner-managed cash chasing
- disconnected tools that hide where money is stuck
