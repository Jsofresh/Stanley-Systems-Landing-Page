# About Stanley Systems

Canonical HTML page: https://stanley-systems.com/about

Stanley Systems exists because service businesses often do strong work in the field and lose money in the office work behind the job.

The leaks are usually not dramatic. They are late invoices, weak handoffs, missed follow-up, quiet review gaps, forgotten referral asks, past customers nobody contacts again, and tools that do not push the work forward.

Stanley Systems helps owners find the leak, pick the highest-value fix, and install the workflow that stops it.
