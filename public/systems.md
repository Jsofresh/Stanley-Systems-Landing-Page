# Systems Stanley Systems Can Build

Canonical HTML page: https://stanley-systems.com/systems

Stanley Systems builds office systems that help service businesses collect cash faster, follow up better, capture reviews and referrals, and keep past customers from going quiet.

Core systems:

- Cashflow Control: billing, invoice movement, payment follow-up, job-detail handoffs, and cash visibility. URL: https://stanley-systems.com/systems/cashflow-control
- Repeat Revenue: missed calls, reviews, referrals, past customers, and repeat work. URL: https://stanley-systems.com/systems/repeat-revenue
- Systems Installation Sprint: the build period where Stanley Systems installs the approved workflow. URL: https://stanley-systems.com/systems-installation-sprint

The system is selected after the Cash Flow Assessment identifies the highest-cost leak.
