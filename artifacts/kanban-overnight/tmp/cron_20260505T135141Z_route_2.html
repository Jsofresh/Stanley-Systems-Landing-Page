Yes. The Workflow Audit is the paid first step. Stanley Systems uses it to find the leak before recommending Cash Flow Collection System, Repeat Revenue System, both, or neither.

No. The Workflow Audit is paid diagnostic work. It protects you from buying the wrong build.

What happens after the audit?+

You get a money leak map, the first fix to make, and a straight recommendation on whether a build makes sense.
