## Conversion strategy

The pricing page should not feel like a software pricing grid. It should feel like a paid diagnostic offer for a service business that suspects cash, customers, or office time are slipping through the cracks.

Primary conversion job:

Get the right owner or office lead to apply for the paid Workflow Audit.

Secondary conversion jobs:

1. Make the money leak feel concrete.
2. Make the audit feel safer than guessing at a build.
3. Explain that package pricing comes after diagnosis.
4. Keep the guarantee bold without promising revenue.
5. Route serious intent to `/contact` in v1.

Do not add self-serve checkout for Cash Flow Collection System, Repeat Revenue System, custom builds, guarantee eligibility, non-approved credit variants, or implementation commitments.

## Customer-facing copy rules

Use these rules for pricing, CTAs, cards, FAQs, payment-adjacent pages, and calculator handoff copy.

1. Lead with money, cash timing, office time, billing speed, owner relief, and fewer dropped follow-ups.
2. Say Stanley Systems every time the company is named.
3. Say Workflow Audit as the paid first step.
4. Say Cash Flow Collection System for money already earned but stuck before collection.
5. Say Repeat Revenue System for money from customers already earned.
6. Avoid unsupported promises. Do not guarantee revenue, profit, new customers, reviews, referrals, or call volume.
7. Do not show package prices in v1.
8. Do not show a Workflow Audit dollar amount unless Jaden provides the approved fee.
9. Keep the guarantee visible near the audit offer and pair it with compact qualification language.
10. Keep every line easy for a plumber, HVAC owner, or marine shop owner to understand in ten seconds.

Forbidden public framing:

1. Technical tools as the hero.
2. Generic process-first language.
3. Software pricing grid language.
4. Free consultation language.
5. Shortened company naming.
6. Any old price test.
7. Any promise that the customer will make a specific amount of money.

Forbidden public words for this pass:

Use the prohibited term list from the Kanban task body. Do not place those terms in customer-facing pricing copy.

## Workflow Audit section draft

Headline:

The paid audit that finds the money leak before the build starts.

Body:

Stanley Systems reviews the office path where money usually gets stuck: completed jobs waiting to be billed, invoices waiting on follow-up, estimates with no next step, old customers nobody has reached back out to, weak review flow, and calls that do not get captured cleanly.

The audit gives you a plain-English map of what is leaking, why it matters, and what should be fixed first.

What the audit checks:

1. Finished work that has not turned into collected cash.
2. Jobs that are complete but not invoice-ready.
3. Open invoices that need better follow-up.
4. Estimates sitting with no next step.
5. Past customers who should be brought back into the loop.
6. Review and referral paths that are not being used.
7. Missed calls or weak call capture.
8. Office handoffs that force the owner or office team to chase details.

What you get:

1. A money leak map.
2. The highest-value first fix.
3. A recommendation for Cash Flow Collection System, Repeat Revenue System, both, or neither.
4. A clear next step if a build makes sense.

## FAQ draft

Question:

Is the Workflow Audit free?

Answer:

No. The Workflow Audit is the paid first step. It is how Stanley Systems finds the money leak before recommending a build.

Question:

Why not just buy the Cash Flow Collection System or Repeat Revenue System now?

Answer:

Because the right fix depends on where the leak is. Some businesses need faster billing and collection. Some need better follow-up with past customers, reviews, referrals, and calls. Some need both. The Workflow Audit decides what is worth building first.

Question:

Do you show package prices on the website?

Answer:

Not in v1. Cash Flow Collection System and Repeat Revenue System are scoped after the Workflow Audit. Stanley Systems does not sell those builds through self-serve checkout.

Question:

What happens if Stanley Systems cannot find a money leak?

Answer:

If your business qualifies and Stanley Systems cannot find one clear money leak we can fix, you get your Workflow Audit fee back.

Question:

Who qualifies for the guarantee?

Answer:

The guarantee is for real service businesses with enough job, invoice, customer, call, estimate, or review volume for leaks to matter. Stanley Systems also needs access to the relevant systems and a reachable decision maker or operations contact during the audit.

Question:

Does the guarantee mean Stanley Systems guarantees more revenue?

Answer:

No. The guarantee means Stanley Systems will find one clear money leak we can fix, or qualified businesses get the Workflow Audit fee back. It does not guarantee revenue, profit, customers, reviews, referrals, call volume, or a no-cost system.

Question:

What kinds of businesses are a good fit?

Answer:

Service businesses with a real office function and a real software stack are the best fit. That usually means a field, job, dispatch, CRM, billing, or accounting system, plus office work that slows down billing, collection, customer follow-up, or owner time.

Question:

What if we already use QuickBooks, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Jobber, or another field system?

Answer:

That is often the point. Stanley Systems works around the systems the business already uses and looks for the places where job, billing, customer, and follow-up details still get stuck.

Question:

Is this a software migration?

Answer:

No. Stanley Systems is not trying to move your business to a new platform. The audit looks at the office-side gaps inside your current setup.

Question:

Where does the Workflow Audit lead?

Answer:

It can lead to Cash Flow Collection System, Repeat Revenue System, both, or neither. If a build is not worth it, the audit should say that.

## Objection handling draft

Objection:

We already have software for this.

Response:

Good. Stanley Systems is built for businesses that already have real systems. The audit looks for the office gaps that still slow billing, collection, customer follow-up, and owner time after the software is already in place.

Objection:

I do not want another platform.

Response:

That is not the goal. Stanley Systems looks at the workflow around the tools you already use and finds where money, customers, or office time are still leaking.

Objection:

Why do I have to pay for the audit?

Response:

Because the audit is the work that finds the leak. It protects you from buying the wrong build and gives you a clear first fix even if Stanley Systems does not build the rest.

Objection:

Can you just tell me the package price?

Response:

Not before diagnosis. Cash Flow Collection System and Repeat Revenue System depend on the leak, the systems involved, the volume, and the access needed. The Workflow Audit decides the right scope first.

Objection:

What if you do not find anything?

Response:

If your business qualifies and Stanley Systems cannot find one clear money leak we can fix, you get the Workflow Audit fee back.

Objection:

Will this bring in guaranteed revenue?

Response:

No. Stanley Systems does not guarantee revenue, profit, customers, reviews, referrals, or call volume. The work is about finding and fixing clear office-side money leaks.

Objection:

My office person already handles this.

Response:

The question is how much paid office time is being spent chasing the same details every week. The audit shows whether that time is protecting revenue or covering for a leak that should be fixed.

Objection:

We are too small.

Response:

Maybe. The best fit is a service business with enough jobs, invoices, customers, calls, estimates, or reviews for leaks to matter. If there is no real office workflow to inspect, Stanley Systems may not be the right fit yet.
